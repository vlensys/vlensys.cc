shoegaze and tnnan

^

ohhh shoegaze, how much i love it!!! personally, i think i'd consider it to be top 3 genres oat (and it aint 2 or 3) i love how diverse the sound is, so many bands with their own unique sound.

i myself got introdced to shoegaze with the album "the now now and never" by what is your name (it's also my tiktok/discord pfp!). the album is fully instrumental (not including samples/the first song's beginning lol) and it's a mix of post punk and shoegaze!

for me, my fav song is most defo "youthful days", i love it's angely voice sample that they used and the relaxing guitar. my fav part of the song is probably right in the middle, at around 4:00, where the song changes to mostly just the guitar loop, bass and voice. i adore this part because of how calm and relaxing/catchy it is, feels like walking my dog at 10pm on a hot summer day (maybe because thats exactly how i discovered the album....). after this part it fades into another part with a new guitar (or is that keyboard? dunno) loop and then the old loop fades right back in, combining basically every loop that was in the song. i don't know exactly why, but the reason i like this song so much is probably because of how it reminds me of my childhood. dunno why it does, it just does! and i think thats why i love it sm
